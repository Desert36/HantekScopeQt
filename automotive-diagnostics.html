Car Diagnostics with an Oscilloscope

HantekScopeQt is affordable, high-performance automotive diagnosis software for professional technicians and enthusiasts. It turns a compatible oscilloscope into a complete car diagnosis platform with guided tests, crank & cam correlation and network decoding — performance comparable to Pico and Autel.

Diagnostic Capabilities

  • 185+ guided automotive diagnostics
  • Misfire diagnosis with ignition waveform analysis
  • Relative compression testing (current clamp)
  • Injector analysis (current and voltage waveforms)
  • Ignition analysis (primary and secondary)
  • Charging and starting system analysis
  • Crankshaft & camshaft correlation
  • Pressure transducer support
  • Intermittent fault recording (unlimited streaming)

CAN Bus Decoder & Protocols

HantekScopeQt includes built-in CAN bus decoder software and supports the most common automotive networks:

  • CAN
  • CAN FD
  • J1939
  • LIN
  • SENT (Fast)
  • PSI5
  • I²C / I²S
  • 1-Wire
  • 10BASE-T1S

How Misfire Diagnosis Works

  1. Connect your oscilloscope to the circuit under test
  2. Capture the waveform in HantekScopeQt
  3. Compare against known-good reference waveforms
  4. Use crank & cam correlation for timing faults
  5. Export or share the capture for reporting

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best automotive diagnosis software?

For oscilloscope-based diagnostics, HantekScopeQt is an affordable, high-performance option with 185+ guided automotive tests, crank and cam correlation and protocol decoding on Windows, Linux and Android.

Can an oscilloscope diagnose an engine misfire?

Yes. An oscilloscope is one of the most reliable ways to diagnose misfires. HantekScopeQt provides ignition waveform analysis, relative compression testing and injector analysis to pinpoint the cause.

Does HantekScopeQt decode CAN bus signals?

Yes. HantekScopeQt decodes CAN, CAN FD, J1939, LIN, SENT, PSI5, I²C, I²S, 1-Wire and 10BASE-T1S signals.
