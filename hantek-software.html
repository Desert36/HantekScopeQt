The Complete Hantek Software

HantekScopeQt is affordable, high-performance Hantek software for the most popular Hantek USB oscilloscopes. Whether you own a Hantek 6022BE, 1008, 6082, 6102 or 6212, HantekScopeQt gives you modern acquisition, waveform analysis and professional automotive diagnostics — performance comparable to Pico and Autel at a fraction of the cost.

It is also a modern OpenHantek and OpenHantek6022 alternative, supporting the same 6022 series hardware with a richer feature set.

Frequently Asked Questions

What software works with Hantek oscilloscopes?

HantekScopeQt supports the Hantek 6022BE, 6022BL, 1008, 1008C, 6052, 6082, 6102 and 6212 series on Windows, Linux and Android, with waveform import/export, automotive diagnostics and crank and cam correlation.

Is HantekScopeQt an OpenHantek alternative?

Yes. HantekScopeQt is a modern alternative to OpenHantek and OpenHantek6022, supporting the same Hantek 6022 hardware with additional features such as waveform conversion, protocol decoding and guided diagnostics.

Does HantekScopeQt work on Windows 11?

Yes. HantekScopeQt runs on Windows 11 and Windows 10, as well as Linux and Android.
